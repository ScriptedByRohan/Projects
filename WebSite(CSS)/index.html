About Us

Welcome to Gotham City, a metropolis defined by its towering art-deco skyline, rain-slicked alleyways, and the eternal struggle between light and shadow. Behind the facade of this sprawling urban landscape lies a city pushed to the brink by corruption, crime, and the colorful chaos of its most notorious villains. But where there is darkness, there is also a beacon of hope. This website is the ultimate digital chronicle of Gotham City and its legendary protector, The Batman.

Batman

The Batman is Gotham’s ultimate protector—a symbol of fear to the superstitious and cowardly, and a beacon of hope to a city trapped in darkness. Born out of tragedy, the Dark Knight has dedicated his life to an endless war on crime. Lacking supernatural powers, he relies on unmatched intellect, peak human conditioning, and an arsenal of cutting-edge technology to achieve the impossible: protecting Gotham City from itself.
